# Competitor Coin Sinks in Cross-Stitch and Color-by-Number Mobile Apps (2024–2026)

Method note: Research done 2026-11-01 via web search + App Store listing fetches (US storefront). Google Play pages could not be fetched (truncated), so Android-only review text is mostly from search snippets. App Store "version history" often omits the year for the latest ~12 months; entries without a year are most likely 2026. Reddit threads did not surface in search; no Sensor Tower/AppMagic coverage of coin sinks found. Store listings rarely state what coins buy, so many sink descriptions come from user reviews (anecdotal) or IAP item names (inference).

## Q1. Which top apps exist and what do their coins/gems buy?

### Takeaway
In cross-stitch apps the dominant coin sink is **unlocking patterns / pattern packs / collections**; booster-style sinks (hints, bombs, magic wand) appear in the newer entrants (Cross Stitch Party, Playcus). Big color-by-number apps (Happy Color, Wildlife Color by Number) have moved toward subscriptions + ad removal, with coins mainly for hints/tools. Only one cross-stitch competitor found (Cross Stitch Party, ZipoApps) has a decoration meta-game ("Decor Room", added Dec 2024).

### Inferences
- Sink taxonomy actually observed: (1) pattern/pack/collection/album unlock — near-universal in cross-stitch; (2) consumable boosters: hint, bomb (area fill), magic wand / paint bucket (fill all of one color), brushes; (3) story/chapter unlock (TAPCLAP, Playcus); (4) frames/backgrounds/fabric color cosmetics (TAPCLAP, Cross Stitch Room); (5) decor room meta (Cross Stitch Party); (6) mystery patterns (Cross Stitch Party); (7) replay finished puzzle (Appynation).
- Photo-to-pattern is a subscription perk (Eyewind), not a coin sink, in what was found.
- Trend: the largest color-by-number apps (Happy Color, Wildlife) are de-emphasizing coin gates in favor of subscriptions + licensed collections.

### Gaps
- No listing for exact titles "Stitch Master" or "Cross-Stitch Gold" found. (Project CLAUDE.md notes CrossCraft lives at /Volumes/ssd/react_native_workspace/stitch-master — inspect locally for that one.)
- Coin prices of individual items (hint cost, pack cost in coins) are almost never published; only review anecdotes (TAPCLAP 50/100-coin pictures; Eyewind ≥1,500-coin collection).
- Paint by Number (other publishers), Color Planet, Jigsaw-puzzle-like hybrids: no coin-sink detail found.
- No evidence found of streak freezes, season passes, gifting coins to friends, or custom palettes bought with coins in these apps.

## Q2. Price points vs earning rates

### Takeaway
Real-money coin price is roughly $2–$4.50 per 1,000 coins in most cross-stitch apps (wildly different coin scales though). The recurring imbalance: completion rewards return ~40% of an item's cost, so the free loop cannot sustain unlocks.

### Cited Findings
- TAPCLAP: 50-coin picture returns 20 coins; 100-coin picture returns 40 coins (2022 review) — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/cross-stitch-masters/id1467166931). Coins cost ≈$4.90–$7.98 per 1,000 (computed from IAP list).
- Eyewind: collection completion ≈1,000 coins vs next collection ≥1,500 — [Google Play snippet](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.inapp.cross.stitch&hl=en_US)
- ZipoApps per-1,000-coin cost: $3.98 (500 pack) down to $2.00 (10,000 pack) — computed from [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/-/id6477046090)
- Cross-Stitch World: ≈$1.15–$1.71 per 1,000 Cash (computed) — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/cross-stitch-world/id1034256780)
- Cross Stitch Room: $14.99–$20 per 1,000 coins (small coin scale) — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/-/id1277235416)
- Cross Stitch Joy wheel: 60 coins per spin — [Gamebrain](https://gamebrain.co/game/cross-stitch-joy)
- TAPCLAP daily Facebook share: 5 coins (2022 review) — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/cross-stitch-masters/id1467166931)
- Eyewind App Store reviewer asked for higher completion rewards and larger daily bonus — [App Store GB](https://apps.apple.com/gb/app/cross-stitch-color-by-number/id1246165006)

### Inferences
- An item-cost:completion-reward ratio near 2.5:1 is perceived as "forcing real money"; Cross-Stitch World's praised economy suggests rewards closer to pack cost.

### Gaps
- No systematic earning-rate data (coins/hour) for any app.

## Q3. What players praise or complain about

### Takeaway
Praise: generous currency that lets you buy packs you want (Cross-Stitch World), plentiful free hints, tools like bucket/hint. Complaints: low completion payouts vs prices, paid packs vanishing after updates, coin penalties for mistakes, ads tied to rewards, games stalling once coins run out.

### Cited Findings
- Praise: "It gives you enough to buy the packs that you want!" (2020); "The currency in this game is actually great to work with" — pack prices reasonable, completion pays well (2024-07-31) — [App Store, Cross-Stitch World](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/cross-stitch-world/id1034256780)
- Complaint: Cross-Stitch World packs overpriced relative to coins per pattern; pack prices rose while payouts fell — [App Store reviews (iPad)](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/cross-stitch-world/id1034256780?see-all=reviews&platform=ipad)
- Complaint: ad-removal pitched via cash pack purchase, resentment at repeat upsell (2022) — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/cross-stitch-world/id1034256780)
- Complaint: Eyewind packs bought with coins disappeared after a May 2023 update; later restored — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/cross-stitch-color-by-number/id1246165006)
- Complaint: TAPCLAP game stalls when coins run out; reviewer would prefer one-time fee to unlock all; 2025 reviewer says story rewards pointless and buying coins for pictures defeats the purpose — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/cross-stitch-masters/id1467166931); [Google Play snippet](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.tapclap.crossstitch&hl=en_US)
- Complaint: Cross Stitch Quest mistake → fewer coins is unfair (accessibility, 2021) — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id1444986176)
- Complaint: Playcus forced reward ads even when declined (2023); ads to resume a started picture (2019) — [App Store Craft & Art](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id1660005840); [App Store Coloring Art](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id1413137373)
- Complaint: Wildlife rewarded ad stops halfway, losing reward (2025-01-23); praise for hint/paint bucket tools — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/color-by-number-coloring-games/id1317978215)
- Praise: Happy Color "no hidden fees", bonus pictures without Facebook — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/happy-color-color-by-number/id1407852246)
- Cross Stitch Joy: players liked graphics but disliked monetization — [Gamebrain](https://gamebrain.co/game/cross-stitch-joy)

### Inferences
- Players in this genre (relaxation, older demographic) react badly to sinks that block finishing a started picture or punish mistakes; they accept sinks for *more content* if earn rates are visible and fair.
- Purchase durability (packs surviving updates/subscription expiry) is a trust issue that surfaces repeatedly.

### Gaps
- Reddit (r/CrossStitch, r/mobilegaming) sentiment not retrieved; most review quotes are pre-2024.

## Q4. Novel sinks (meta-game, showcase, gifting, coins vs ads)

### Takeaway
Novel sinks in the space are thin: Decor Room + Mystery Patterns + Books (Cross Stitch Party), stories/chapters (TAPCLAP, Playcus), streak-hatched pet (Wildlife), friends/levelling/video-share (Cross-Stitch World), replay-for-coins (Appynation). Nobody found sells gallery showcase or friend-gifting for coins.

### Cited Findings
- Decor Room, Mystery Patterns, new rooms, Books, Shop — [App Store, Cross Stitch Party](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/-/id6477046090)
- Stories unlocked with earned tokens — [App Store, Cross-Stitch Masters](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/cross-stitch-masters/id1467166931)
- Pet egg hatches at 7-day streak — [App Store, Wildlife](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/color-by-number-coloring-games/id1317978215)
- Friends system, levelling, share-as-video — [App Store, Cross-Stitch World](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/cross-stitch-world/id1034256780)
- Spend coins to replay solved puzzles — [Appynation](https://appynation.helpshift.com/hc/en/20-picture-cross-color/faq/404-what-are-coins-used-for-1724252555/)
- "Gift" IAP $4.99 (contents unspecified) — [App Store, Eyewind](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/cross-stitch-color-by-number/id1246165006)
- Watch ad to triple completion reward (Playcus) — [App Store snippet](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/cross-stitch-coloring-book/id1499339385)
- Hint ads persist after ad-removal ("Remove ads (hint ads stay)") — i.e. hint sink is ad-funded — [App Store, Happy Color](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/happy-color-color-by-number/id1407852246)

### Inferences
- White space: a coin-funded decoration/display room for finished stitches, thread/fabric/hoop cosmetics, streak freezes, and gifting patterns to friends are largely unoccupied in cross-stitch competitors.

### Gaps
- Could not confirm whether Cross Stitch Party's Decor Room is paid with coins, or the price of decor items.
- No data on season passes in this genre.
