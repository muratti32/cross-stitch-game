# Player Desires & Delight Moments in Cozy/Creative Mobile Games (coin-sink candidates)

Method note: Reddit (reddit.com) could not be reached. The search tool's user agent is blocked there, so there are **no Reddit quotes** in these notes. Player voices come mainly from App Store review pages for: Cross-Stitch: Color by Number (id1246165006), Stitch. by Logically Fun (id1581052096) and Cross-Stitch World (id1034256780). Third-party summaries supplement them. Many App Store reviews are older than 2023; each quote carries its date. Some aggregator sites (kimola, marlvel.ai) look like automated or report-selling pages, so they are flagged or left out.

## What features do players request in reviews and forums?

### Takeaway
The requests that come up most are for collection and content: more patterns, a "finished" folder or gallery, going back to finished hoops, search, and themed sections. Next come creative-control requests: free play or free stitch, re-coloring, own-photo import and choosing pattern sizes. Quality-of-life asks (zoom, dark mode, volume sliders) are frequent too. Requests for print/DMC export show up in the *pattern-maker* app category rather than in game reviews. A competing game now offers a printable PDF pattern as a completion reward.

**Social**

### Inferences
- The gallery or "finished folder" is a recurring request that players treat as basic. Charging coins for the basic gallery would likely annoy them. *Cosmetic upgrades* to it are better coin sinks: frames, hoop styles, fabric backgrounds, wall/room display slots.
- Players already want cosmetic, non-functional choices: re-coloring finished work, thread textures, a flat vs animated look, diamond squares vs crosses. These are natural coin sinks because they never block progress.
- Photo-to-pattern is already gated behind coins or subscription in a leading competitor. Players accept this, but it competes with dedicated free or paid pattern-maker apps.
- No player in the reviews read asked for a time-lapse replay. It is a strong *marketing/share* feature in pixel-art apps, and players notice when it breaks. Treat it as a delight feature to unlock with coins (e.g., HD export or no watermark), not a proven request.

## What delight moments are frequently praised?

### Takeaway
Players praise tactile satisfaction most (sound, haptics, the moment a segment completes) along with calm, ad-free sessions and steady new content. Players also call earned in-game currency "not stingy" or "reasonable" approvingly, so generosity itself delights.

### Cited Findings
- "It also is satisfying when you complete a segment of the hoop." (Stitch., 11/17/2024) — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/stitch/id1581052096?see-all=reviews)
- "some of the most satisfying sound effects imaginable" (Stitch., "A masterclass in casual game design", 02/28/2024) — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/stitch/id1581052096?see-all=reviews)
- "I sometimes find myself just looking at the background behind the hoop" (Stitch., Feb 3). Players value ambient visuals. — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/stitch/id1581052096?see-all=reviews)
- "finishing one of the puzzles without using hints" gives a scoring bonus that players enjoy (Stitch., 03/14/2024). Another player proposed "Record the time needed to complete a hoop for the first time and remove hints" (11/07/2022). — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/stitch/id1581052096?see-all=reviews)
- "jump right back in to wherever you left off even if you completely close the game" (Stitch., 03/14/2024) — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/stitch/id1581052096?see-all=reviews)
- "I have played for hours and have seen no ads, nothing to disrupt the calming effects." (Cross-Stitch World, 03/30/2023); "this game helps me to calm down and relax" (04/15/2025) — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/cross-stitch-world/id1034256780?see-all=reviews)
- "this game isn't stingy with in-game money" (12/18/2020); "you can buy packs of designs for a reasonable price (not actual money)" (07/31/2024) (both Cross-Stitch World) — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/cross-stitch-world/id1034256780?see-all=reviews)
- "it takes many brain away from the problems at hand" (Cross-Stitch: Color by Number, 11/05/2018); "I've been using this app for 5 years now and love it" (08/13/2023) — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/cross-stitch-color-by-number/id1246165006?see-all=reviews)
- Haptics can backfire: "That's a battery killer since each correct patch triggers the haptics twice." (Stitch., 10/31/2022) — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/stitch/id1581052096?see-all=reviews)
- "Zero anxiety by design. No timers, no lives, no fail states." is used as a selling point (Cross Stitch: Antistress listing) — [AppGoblin](https://appgoblin.info/apps/6769534330)

### Inferences
- Sensory polish (SFX, haptics, an ambient background) is the core delight. Selling *variations* for coins, such as ambient scenes or background sets and thread-sound or texture packs, builds on something players already love. Settings toggles must stay free.
- Hint-free completion bonuses and a personal-best time are wanted as optional challenge. They could pay coin rewards (a coin *source*), which helps balance coin sinks.
- Collection completion and an end-of-pattern celebration are expected in the genre, but no direct player quotes were found praising a completion *animation* specifically.

### Gaps
- No direct quotes praising completion animations, collection-completion rewards or a replay of one's own stitching in cross-stitch games specifically.

## Real-world crossover: do digital players also stitch physically and want printable charts/kits?

### Takeaway
There is real crossover. Reviews mention current real stitchers, lapsed stitchers and people who can no longer stitch for health reasons. A 2026 competitor gives away a **free printable PDF pattern with DMC palette as a completion reward**. No data was found on what share of players stitch physically.

### Cited Findings
- "As a person who did the real thing (counted cross stitch) for many years ... I found this game to be a look back at how much I enjoyed doing it back then." (Cross-Stitch: Color by Number, 06/07/2018) — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/cross-stitch-color-by-number/id1246165006?see-all=reviews)
- "I have vision and joint issues so no longer have the ability to do real cross stitch or embroidery, which I loved." (Cross-Stitch World, 11/12/2021) — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/cross-stitch-world/id1034256780?see-all=reviews)
- "I've been hyper-fixating on cross stitch irl" (Stitch., 10/30/2022); "Benefits of being digital not physical, lol" (12/19/2022) — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/stitch/id1581052096?see-all=reviews)
- "it reminds me so much more of diamond painting" (Cross-Stitch World, 03/30/2023) — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/cross-stitch-world/id1034256780?see-all=reviews)
- Cross Stitch: Antistress (Crimson Pine Games, released around Jul 2026): "Complete an artwork in the app" to "earn its free printable PDF pattern" for physical stitching, with "DMC thread palettes". Its subscription costs from $14.99/month, and revenue is estimated at under $10K/month. These are the developer's claims plus a tracker estimate. — [AppGoblin](https://appgoblin.info/apps/6769534330)
- Real stitchers' pattern-maker apps compete on print/export. Stitchly offers "print directly from Stitchly with a range of options and layout guides" or "export your final pattern into a PDF or JPG", thread brands "DMC, Anchor, Weeks Dye Works, Kreinik", and lets you "limit the thread shades required too, to keep within your budget" (World of Cross Stitching, Oct 21, 2024). — [World of Cross Stitching](https://worldofcrossstitching.com/how-to-cross-stitch/stitchly-app)
- A Stitchly user praised the export because it includes the number of skeins needed. Cross Stitch Creator supports 489 DMC colors and patterns up to 800x800. Stitch Fiddle exports a PDF legend with DMC codes and stitch counts. — [AppAdvice Stitchly](https://appadvice.com/app/stitchly-cross-stitch/1467867656); [Cross Stitch Creator](https://apps.apple.com/app/id897287820); [Stitch Fiddle](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6759521479)

### Inferences
- A "printable chart + DMC thread list (with skein counts)" for a completed pattern is a credible premium coin sink. Real stitchers already pay for this in tool apps, and one game competitor already uses it as a hook, though as a *free* reward. Charging coins for it is plausible but risks unfavorable comparison with that competitor.
- Accessibility is a key motive (lapsed or physically limited stitchers). Large zoom, contrast and color-blind aids are a must-have and should not be paywalled.

### Gaps
- No survey data on what percentage of digital cross-stitch players stitch physically or buy kits.
- No evidence on whether players would buy *physical kits* through an app.
- r/CrossStitch discussion of digital games could not be accessed (Reddit blocked).

## Pain points around currency (too few coins, ad walls) to avoid

### Takeaway
The biggest pain point across color-by-number, jigsaw and cross-stitch apps is ad intrusion: ads that are too long, unskippable, stacked or hard to exit. Next is devaluation: packs getting pricier or coin rewards being cut. Worst of all is losing purchased or earned content. Players accept watching optional ads for coins, and some ask for *bigger* rewards per ad.

### Cited Findings
- "I would certainly watch more ads for even 100 coins." and "should offer coin variety for each one, so instead of just 50 coins each time" (Cross-Stitch: Color by Number, 01/17/2025). Players accept rewarded ads but feel the reward is too small. — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/cross-stitch-color-by-number/id1246165006?see-all=reviews)
- "I lost the money from purchasing the coins for these packs." (05/24/2023); "I have lost all of the packages I have rightfully bought with quite a few coins I have earned" (06/04/2023); "There is 35 picture packages I can now buy, and that is all." (05/18/2023) (all Cross-Stitch: Color by Number) — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/cross-stitch-color-by-number/id1246165006?see-all=reviews)
- "I have invited 8 people to receive the 600 points and I didn't receive the points." (04/10/2018). A referral reward that never arrived. — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/cross-stitch-color-by-number/id1246165006?see-all=reviews)
- "the coin reward system has changed and you now have more coins than you could ever want" (12/06/2022). Inflation can make coins meaningless. The same player pays for the subscription. — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/cross-stitch-color-by-number/id1246165006?see-all=reviews)
- "But the packs got more expensive and paid out less cash to buy more." (Cross-Stitch World, 12/04/2017); "Please lower the prices" (08/28/2018); "But now there are ads! I never saw them before." (06/29/2022); "an add pops up and plays all the way through it won't let you hit continue" (04/15/2025) — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/cross-stitch-world/id1034256780?see-all=reviews)
- Happy Color: an Apple forum post from April 2024 says the app "is taken over by ads that contain other ads and don't allow getting back to the app". A summary says ads "often exceeding 30 seconds" are "detrimental to the app's relaxing nature". A Play reviewer wrote "the app is a 5, but the ads are making me crazy". — [Apple Discussions](https://discussions.apple.com/thread/255591677); [Gamebrain](https://gamebrain.co/game/happy-color-coloring-book)
- Eyewind's Cross Stitch: Color by Number drew criticism for "the overwhelming number of ads that disrupt gameplay" (aggregator summary). — [Gamebrain](https://gamebrain.co/game/cross-stitch-color-by-number)
- Jigsaw apps: reviewers say pop-ups cut their play time, ads come "almost nonstop", and the developers "reduced coin rewards", making coins "useless". In Jigsolitaire coins have no clear use, which confuses players. — [Jigsaw Puzzle App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/jigsaw-puzzle/id495583717); [Gamebrain Jigsolitaire](https://gamebrain.co/game/jigsolitaire)

### Inferences
- Design rules that follow: (1) rewarded ads are opt-in only and pay a meaningful amount (players named ~100 coins as a fair benchmark against 50); (2) never devalue coins or raise pack prices after launch; (3) purchases and unlocks must be server-backed and restorable; (4) referral and invite rewards must reliably arrive; (5) every coin sink should have a clear, visible purpose; (6) avoid inflation that makes coins pointless.
- Players who say "not stingy" stay for years. Generous earning combined with desirable cosmetic sinks beats scarcity.

### Gaps
- No quantitative data (e.g., survey percentages) found on cozy players' tolerance for ads versus IAP. Bryter's 2025 women gamers study covers only PC/console, with no mobile or spend data. No Quantic Foundry 2024 cozy-specific breakdown was found.
