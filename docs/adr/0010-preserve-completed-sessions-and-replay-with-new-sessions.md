# Preserve completed sessions and replay with new sessions

A player may have at most one active Stitching Session for a given Pattern. Session Completion is local-first: the device atomically closes it at its final progress revision even offline and records an idempotent completion for later backend validation. The first accepted valid completion becomes the terminal server revision and read-only durable history. A Late Progress Operation uploaded by another device is acknowledged as superseded and removed locally after that device adopts the completed snapshot; it cannot reopen the attempt or become an implicit replay. Undo remains free while a session is active but cannot reopen a completed attempt. Choosing to play the Pattern again creates a new Replay Session with a new operation sequence instead of resetting or overwriting the completed one. We accept additional session rows, stale-operation acknowledgements, rare superseded Undo intent, offline completion reconciliation, and an explicit read-only boundary in exchange for unambiguous completion history, idempotent future rewards, and replay behavior that never destroys an achievement the player already earned.
