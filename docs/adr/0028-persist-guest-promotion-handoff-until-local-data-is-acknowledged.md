# Persist Guest Promotion Handoff until local data is acknowledged

Before requesting Guest Promotion Preview, the device creates Guest Promotion Manifest containing stable identifiers, local versions, and checksums for every local Session Progress, Session Completion, Pending Coin Reward, and Pattern Like record. Each pending reward also includes complete source, Session, Pattern, monotonic-event, and state-transition evidence. The backend validates that evidence, combines the manifest with current Guest Ledger and relevant target-account state, and binds the preview to all versions plus the validation result. Before confirming it, the device writes a durable Promotion Handoff containing the preview-approved transfer-or-discard disposition for every manifest item and the locally prepared package identifier and checksum. A changed local item, validation result, Guest Ledger, target promotion state, or target Unlock state makes the preview stale.

Confirmation acquires a short-lived Promotion Commit Lock. The client remains in the transfer flow, and the locked Guest identity cannot start gameplay, mutate its ledger, reconcile rewards, change Likes, or start a second promotion. It then uploads an immutable Promotion Transfer Package containing the complete local records, stable identifiers, dispositions, and checksum but excluding Offline Pattern Data. The backend validates the package's schema, identities, checksum, manifest completeness, target state, and preview freshness, then durably stages it. A failed upload, stale state, cancellation, or lease expiry releases the lock, leaves the Guest session unchanged, and deletes uncommitted staging immediately when possible and no later than 24 hours.

Only after staging succeeds may a serializable compare-and-swap commit consume the guest identity. It revalidates the target economic-promotion and relevant Unlock versions and atomically resolves every economic disposition: Coin, Unlocks, eligible or discarded Pending Coin Rewards, completion-reward deduplication, Guest Ledger closure, and the target economy slot. If a concurrent operation changed eligibility, commit fails stale and requires a new preview and confirmation rather than silently changing promotion mode. A successful commit is the irreversible boundary: the target account becomes immediately usable, the promotion cannot be cancelled or rolled back, and the consumed guest identity cannot be reactivated. The backend retains and processes only unresolved progress, completion-history, and Like items from the staged package, independently of the app or source device. If the app closes or crashes, the local handoff resumes status reconciliation under the target account rather than attempting another promotion; if the app is deleted or the device is lost, the durable server package still completes the transfer.

Each remaining non-economic staged record is processed through an idempotent backend operation, and its local source is retained until transfer or intentional discard is durably acknowledged according to the preview. Economic source items may be removed as soon as the commit acknowledgement records their terminal disposition. Unresolved progress, history, and Like items appear as Syncing, retry automatically, and expose a manual retry action. After bounded retries, the handoff enters Promotion Needs Attention, retains its server payload, alerts operations, and exposes support rather than expiring, dropping data, or returning the account to Guest mode. Promotion-Locked Offline Pattern Data is never uploaded: after backend commit it is deleted before target-account gameplay because the account lacks the required Unlock, while its progress record remains protected in the staged package and can later resume through Session Preparation after purchase. Acknowledged payload is deleted item by item; when nothing remains, the server package and local handoff are removed and only a minimal checksum and idempotency receipt remains.

We accept a local manifest and handoff journal, a short mutation pause, optimistic target-state revalidation, temporary pre-commit staging, durable post-commit staging, duplicate transfer bytes, package validation and expiry, per-item acknowledgements, operational alerts, crash recovery, partial-transfer UI, automatic and manual retries, and an irreversible post-commit boundary in exchange for one exact preview, no post-snapshot Guest writes, and no failure window where the backend consumes the Guest identity but app deletion or device loss silently loses progress or social data. The package is bounded migration state, not a general device backup, a pattern-artifact upload, a rollback mechanism, or a way to reactivate a consumed identity.
