# Generate AI artwork with fal.ai and purchased credits

AI Artwork generation uses fal.ai, a paid external service. Every request reserves one unit from the Registered Account's available AI Credit balance, which may contain purchased or Membership Credit Grant value; only successful AI Artwork Delivery captures it, while a reconciled terminal failure or safety rejection releases it. Any Registered Account may buy and spend AI Credit; Premium Membership is not an additional prerequisite, avoiding a subscription-plus-consumable double gate. Pattern Conversion itself remains unlimited and does not consume AI Credit; the paid operation is successfully delivering Source Artwork, not turning approved artwork into a playable Pattern. We accept dependence on fal.ai's availability, pricing, and API behavior in exchange for using its managed image-generation infrastructure instead of operating a model ourselves.

An AI Generation Request reserves its required AI Credit before contacting fal.ai. A client disconnect, API timeout, or missed callback does not release the reservation because fal.ai may still be processing the job. AI Artwork Delivery occurs only after output bytes are copied into game-owned private storage and the AI Artwork Library record is durably committed; that terminal transition captures the reservation exactly once. Only backend reconciliation with a provider terminal failure, safety rejection, or unusable result releases it. A late webhook reconciles idempotently against the original Processing Job and can complete delivery after the client has left, but can never create another artwork or charge. A usable result remains charged even when the player dislikes or declines it, because the external generation cost has already been incurred; generating another result is a new paid request.

Account Deletion Request may cancel an AI job and release its reservation only before the job is submitted to fal.ai. Once submitted, provider reconciliation remains allowed during Deletion Recovery Window and normally ends in ordinary Delivery or proven terminal failure. Delivered artwork returns to the library if deletion is cancelled and is erased by Account Deletion Finalization. If a provider job is exceptionally still unresolved when finalization occurs, a minimal deletion-reconciliation tombstone replaces the account job, the reservation is extinguished with the deleted balance, and any later output is discarded rather than becoming AI Artwork, a charge, or value claimable by another account. A late provider success is never converted into free artwork by the closure flow.

A result marked unsafe by fal.ai's safety checker is a Safety-Rejected Generation, not usable AI Artwork: it is not copied into game storage, the AI Credit Reservation is released, and the request is not retried automatically. The player is never charged for an image the game refuses to deliver.

Before reserving AI Credit or calling fal.ai, the game backend runs a Prompt Safety Check through OpenAI's `omni-moderation-latest` model and blocks disallowed prompts without fal.ai generation cost. The moderation integration stays behind a game-owned service boundary so provider details do not enter the AI generation or credit domain. fal.ai's enabled output safety checker remains a second independent gate; preflight acceptance never makes an output automatically safe.

The first release treats the moderation response's top-level `flagged` boolean as the Prompt Safety Check decision and does not invent category-score thresholds without product-specific evaluation data. A flagged prompt becomes a Prompt Safety Rejection before any credit reservation or generation call; an unflagged prompt may proceed to the independent fal.ai output gate.

All AI attempts, including Prompt Safety Rejections, count toward a per-Registered-Account limit of ten attempts in a rolling ten-minute window. Exceeding the limit produces a normal temporary rate-limit response until the window clears; automated moderation alone never causes permanent account suspension. The values are operational defaults that may be tuned without changing the domain model.

The first release uses fal.ai's `fal-ai/flux-2/turbo` text-to-image endpoint with an approximately one-megapixel output budget, one image per request, and the provider safety checker enabled. Players choose one of three Artwork Aspects before generation: Square (`1024×1024`), Portrait 4:3 (`896×1152`), or Landscape 4:3 (`1152×896`). Results are collected through fal.ai's queue/webhook protocol rather than by holding an application request open. Delivery stores and charges the artwork but never starts Pattern Conversion automatically: the player must make an explicit Artwork Approval, after which conversion preserves the selected shape rather than forcing a later crop. Declining or leaving the result keeps it in the private library and does not refund the credit. Each successfully delivered AI Artwork costs exactly one AI Credit. AI Credit is an app-owned consumption unit rather than a direct representation of fal.ai's monetary price; future higher-cost generation profiles may use different credit prices without redefining the first-release unit. Current provider pricing remains an operational input from [fal.ai's model page](https://fal.ai/models/fal-ai/flux-2/turbo), not a value embedded in the domain model.

Premium Membership is not required to use AI Credit, but each normalized, verified paid Membership Period includes exactly one Membership Credit Grant: 3 credits for Weekly, 15 for Monthly after trial conversion and each paid renewal period, and 180 for Annual purchase or renewal period. Store retries, delayed events, and plan changes cannot duplicate the period grant. The full Annual allowance is granted immediately rather than through a custom monthly schedule. A Monthly Trial unlocks the Premium Theme Collection and Premium Daily Coin Claim without granting AI Credit. These granted credits use the same per-artwork consumption unit as separately purchased credits, allowing non-members to buy occasional usage while giving recurring members continuing AI value without letting a no-charge trial create permanent fal.ai-funded value.

Purchased credits and Membership Credit Grants join one non-expiring balance. Unused credits carry across membership periods and remain owned by the Registered Account after cancellation or expiration; the game does not erase paid or previously granted generation value when membership status changes.
