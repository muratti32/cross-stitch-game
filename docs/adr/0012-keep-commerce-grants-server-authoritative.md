# Keep commerce grants server-authoritative

Stitch Coin Packs, AI Credit Packs, Premium Membership purchases, and membership renewals grant value only after the game backend verifies the store transaction. The first successful grant creates permanent Commerce Transaction Binding between that provider transaction and one Registered Account. Repeated client submissions, restores, store notifications, and reconciliation runs for the same account return the recorded result; another account cannot claim, transfer, or merge that transaction. Account Deletion may remove bounded receipt and account detail but retains a minimal one-way provider-transaction tombstone for the life of the service, so a later account cannot regrant it. Deleting the game account does not cancel the underlying platform subscription. Mobile clients may submit purchase evidence and read resulting state, but cannot increment paid balances or activate membership directly.

Subscription events are normalized into provider-verified Membership Periods so upgrades, downgrades, retries, grace periods, billing recovery, and delayed notifications do not double grant or close the wrong entitlement. Each paid period produces exactly one Membership Credit Grant: Weekly periods grant the weekly allowance, the first Monthly grant waits for paid trial conversion and then follows paid monthly periods, and Annual purchase or renewal periods grant the full annual allowance immediately. Premium entitlement is derived separately from the latest verified store status rather than from the mere presence or reversal of one transaction.

A verified refund or chargeback creates one Commerce Reversal for the transaction's original Stitch Coin or AI Credit grant. If the account has already spent that value, the corresponding balance becomes negative and blocks further spending until it covers the next requested cost. The reversal does not remove existing Pattern Unlocks, AI Artwork, Patterns, or Stitching Sessions; preserving user-created and completed state is preferable to a destructive rollback, while the negative balance prevents the refunded value from funding additional consumption.

A refunded or charged-back Membership Period creates one Membership Reversal: only that period's Membership Credit Grant is withdrawn using the same negative AI Credit balance rule. Entitlement is recomputed from current verified store status, so refunding an older period cannot deactivate a newer valid period. Stitch Coin already obtained through earlier Premium Daily Coin Claims is not clawed back, and neither are Pattern Unlocks or content. This avoids retroactively replaying daily account activity while still reversing the directly refunded membership value.

We accept permanent account binding, store-event normalization, wrong-account recovery support, delayed grants during outages, and reconciliation work in exchange for preventing forged balances, cross-account receipt reuse, duplicate membership credits, and plan-change races while preserving valid purchases across devices signed into the same Registered Account.
