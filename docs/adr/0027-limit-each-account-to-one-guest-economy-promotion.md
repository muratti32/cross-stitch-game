# Limit each account to one Guest Economy Promotion

A Registered Account may receive the economic contents of a Guest Ledger exactly once over its lifetime, whether the Guest Data Promotion targets a newly created account or a pre-existing account reached through sign-in. One serializable compare-and-swap commit verifies the source Guest Installation Identity is unconsumed and the account's economic-promotion version matches the confirmed preview, then adds remaining Stitch Coin, unions Pattern Unlocks, validates and grants eligible Pending Coin Rewards, deduplicates completion-reward source keys, closes the Guest Ledger, consumes the account's economic-promotion slot, and consumes the source identity exactly once.

Later Guest Data Promotions into the same account are Guest Data-Only Promotions. They merge backend session identities, device-local Session Progress through Promotion Session Merge, Session Completion history, and Pattern Likes, but they transfer no Guest Coin, Pattern Unlocks, Pending Coin Rewards, or other reward value. The source Guest Ledger is closed. If transferred progress belongs to a non-free Official Pattern that the account has not unlocked, it becomes a Promotion-Locked Session: progress and history remain, Offline Pattern Data is removed during identity switch, and no artifact grant or gameplay is available until the account independently acquires the Pattern Unlock. Session Preparation then restores the same session rather than creating a new one.

Before either promotion mode mutates state, the device inventories local records in Guest Promotion Manifest and the backend produces a Guest Promotion Preview bound to that checksum, the guest identity, target account, economic-promotion version, and relevant Unlock versions. It explicitly lists transferred, locked, and discarded values. The player must confirm that current preview; stale state requires regeneration. The device durably writes Promotion Handoff before confirmation. Confirmation acquires the guest's only Promotion Commit Lock, pauses new Guest gameplay and mutations, and uploads the matching immutable Promotion Transfer Package. The backend may commit only after that package is complete and validated against the manifest and latest relevant target state. A concurrent promotion that wins the economic slot makes this preview stale; it is never silently downgraded to data-only. Cancellation, expiry, or failure before commit releases the lock and leaves the Guest Installation Identity, ledger, and local data unchanged.

The commit resolves every economic disposition atomically, including Coin, Unlocks, eligible or discarded Pending Coin Rewards, completion-reward deduplication, Guest Ledger closure, source consumption, and the target economy slot. Only high-volume non-economic progress, completion-history, and Like records may remain in the durable package as post-commit Syncing work. This prevents an irreversible identity switch from exposing a half-applied economy.

We accept a lifetime account flag, versioned promotion previews, discarded later guest economic value, explicit pre-promotion disclosure, and temporarily inaccessible paid-Pattern progress in exchange for preventing repeated guest identities from bypassing Reward Day limits, farming completion rewards, or funneling unlimited Unlocks into one durable account. Progress and social history remain recoverable without turning repeated guest installations into an economy multiplier.
