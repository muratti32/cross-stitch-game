# Publish each moderated submission as an immutable catalog item

A Personal Pattern remains the player's editable private content, while every Catalog Submission captures a new immutable copy and passes through Catalog Review independently. The snapshot includes required Catalog Submission Metadata—title, description, one Catalog Source Language, one Catalog Category, and at most five operator-managed Catalog Tag codes—and a Publication Rights Declaration in which the submitting Registered Account affirms that it created the content or holds the rights necessary to publish it and accepts the recorded version of Catalog Publication License. The creator retains ownership while granting the game a non-exclusive, worldwide, royalty-free license to host, technically transform, display, and distribute that catalog entry through the app, preview CDN, stable share page, and necessary service providers. Catalog Withdrawal ends new distribution but preserves the limited rights required for existing Stitching Sessions plus backup, moderation, fraud, and legal records; Safety Removal ends player distribution while private evidence remains. The title and description remain in the single language selected by the player and appear with that language label; the first release creates no automatic translations. Tag codes are stable and language-neutral, while their catalog labels are localized and maintained by the operator, enabling cross-language discovery without translating player-authored text. The declaration, license version, account, and submission time are retained with the snapshot and shown to the moderator; they are an attestation rather than automatic proof of ownership. Resubmitting an edited Personal Pattern never changes or revises an earlier Community Pattern; if approved, it becomes a distinct Community Pattern, and every earlier Community Pattern remains independently available in the catalog. This preserves the validity of every moderation decision and keeps public content stable for players, at the accepted cost of required submission metadata, retained rights attestations, a catalog-operation license, centrally managed tag vocabulary, untranslated player-authored text, potentially similar catalog items, and separate public identities for each submission.

After publication, the owner may request a Catalog Metadata Revision limited to title, description, Catalog Source Language, Catalog Category, and Catalog Tags. Each request is a new immutable snapshot: changed text receives automated safety checks, category and tag codes receive deterministic validation, and a human moderator makes the final decision without revalidating unchanged Pattern bytes. A rejection carries a visible Catalog Rejection Reason and requires the owner to choose between one Catalog Metadata Appeal against that exact snapshot or submitting a new revision; creating the new revision permanently waives the earlier appeal right. Appeals are reviewed by a different moderator when operationally possible, and an active appeal blocks another metadata revision until decided. A Community Pattern has at most one pending metadata revision or active metadata appeal at a time, and a pending snapshot cannot be edited. Before a human decision, the owner may irreversibly withdraw it; the proposed values and withdrawal event remain in the private audit history, no appeal right is created, and a new immutable revision may then be submitted. The last accepted metadata remains public while another revision is pending, rejected, or appealed. Acceptance moves the same Community Pattern's current-metadata pointer to the new snapshot and retains all earlier revisions in a private audit history.

Review Hold still permits the owner to propose a metadata correction, but neither submitting nor accepting it independently restores the Pattern; the moderator resolves the revision together with the Post-Publication Review decision. Catalog Withdrawal and Safety Removal prevent new revisions and close pending revisions without publication. Pattern Artifact and Pattern Preview changes are never metadata revisions: they require a new Catalog Submission and, after approval, create a new Community Pattern. We accept a versioned metadata model, state-aware publication rules, and another review and appeal queue in exchange for correcting catalog presentation without breaking stable Pattern identity, links, sessions, or immutable playable bytes.

Account Deletion Request applies Account Closure Hold during Deletion Recovery Window without changing immutable bytes. Cancellation restores the same public entries and identifiers. Account Deletion Finalization applies Catalog Withdrawal to every Community Pattern, closes pending submissions, revisions, and ordinary appeals, and replaces attribution needed by existing sessions with Deleted Creator. The Catalog Publication License then survives only for existing sessions plus bounded backup, moderation, fraud, and legal records; deletion cannot turn previously distributed bytes into unlicensed orphan data.
