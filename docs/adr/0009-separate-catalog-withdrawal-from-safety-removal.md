# Separate voluntary catalog withdrawal from safety removal

A Catalog Withdrawal by the Community Pattern's owner removes it from discovery and prevents new Stitching Sessions but preserves existing sessions and their Offline Pattern Data. A moderator's Safety Removal is a stronger safety and legal action: it invalidates both new and existing sessions and instructs connected devices to delete the affected Offline Pattern Data at their next backend check. We accept that a fully offline device cannot be revoked instantly, while keeping ordinary creator withdrawal from destroying progress belonging to other players.

Account Deletion Finalization applies the Catalog Withdrawal behavior to every Community Pattern owned by the deleted account. Existing sessions retain immutable content under the limited surviving Catalog Publication License, but attribution becomes Deleted Creator and no public profile or creator link remains. This is not Safety Removal and does not destroy another player's legitimate progress.

Pattern Artifacts remain private and require short-lived Artifact Access Grants. After Catalog Withdrawal, the backend may issue a grant only to a player whose existing Stitching Session already references that Community Pattern. Safety Removal stops all new grants and triggers a purge of the public catalog preview from the CDN; connected clients also receive the existing deletion instruction. This makes the stronger removal effective for future online access without pretending that already-offline bytes can be recalled instantly.

Catalog Withdrawal is irreversible for that Community Pattern identity. Republishing the same or edited Personal Pattern requires a new immutable Catalog Submission, a new Catalog Review, and—if approved—a new Community Pattern; the withdrawn item remains only to support sessions that already referenced it.

Every Community Pattern provides an in-game Community Report action. Only a Registered Account may submit a report; a Guest Player who selects the action is prompted to sign in. A report requires one Community Report Reason: Inappropriate or Unsafe Content, Copyright or Publication Rights, Duplicate or Spam, Misleading Title or Tags, or Other. An explanation is optional for the first four reasons and required for Other. Each report is unique per submitting account and open Post-Publication Review, so repeated requests during that case return the existing report rather than inflating the queue. After closure, a new approved metadata state or materially new evidence may create another rate-limited case rather than being blocked forever by the earlier report. A valid report starts or contributes to a human Post-Publication Review; no individual report automatically hides, changes, or removes content.

Only a human moderator may apply Review Hold while a Post-Publication Review is open; report counts and automated thresholds cannot do so. Review Hold temporarily removes the Community Pattern from discovery and prevents new Stitching Sessions but preserves existing sessions and Offline Pattern Data until the final decision. The owner may submit a Catalog Metadata Revision as a proposed correction, but it cannot publish or clear Review Hold on its own; the moderator resolves both records together. If the review finds no violation, the moderator clears the hold, resolves the revision, and restores normal catalog availability. If only the current approved metadata violates policy—for example a confirmed Misleading Title or Tags report the owner declines to correct—the moderator applies Catalog Metadata Remediation: the violating title or description is replaced with a safe neutral default, violating tags are removed, the category may be reassigned, and prior values remain in the private audit history while the Pattern Artifact, Pattern Preview, catalog availability, and existing sessions stay untouched. Remediation mirrors Profile Remediation and has no separate appeal because the owner may still propose compliant values through an ordinary Catalog Metadata Revision. If the review confirms a serious safety, legal, or policy violation, the moderator applies Safety Removal and closes any pending metadata revision without publication. Catalog Withdrawal likewise closes pending revisions, and both Withdrawal and Safety Removal prevent new ones. Applying Review Hold and reaching any final outcome each create a Moderation Notice delivered to the Community Pattern owner through both in-app messaging and email. The notice identifies the affected Pattern and explains the decision reason but never exposes the identity or account details of any reporting player. This keeps temporary and permanent removal authority human-controlled, gives the owner transparent status, protects reporters from retaliation, and limits report spam or coordinated duplicate submissions.

The owner may create one Safety Removal Appeal against the same immutable Community Pattern and moderation record. Safety Removal remains fully effective while the appeal is open: the Pattern stays outside discovery, Artifact Access Grants remain blocked, its public preview remains purged, existing sessions remain inaccessible, and connected devices retain the deletion instruction. The appeal is assigned to a different moderator when operationally possible. Acceptance rescinds the removal, restores catalog discovery and artifact grants, republishes the Pattern Preview, and allows existing sessions to download Offline Pattern Data again. An upheld removal is final for that Community Pattern. We accept retaining the private origin artifact and moderation evidence during this process so an erroneous removal can be reversed without weakening protection while the appeal is unresolved.
