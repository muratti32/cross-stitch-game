# Separate AI credit from gameplay currency

> Guest economy details are governed by ADR-0026. Guest Stitch Coin and Pattern Unlocks are server-authoritative in an installation-scoped Guest Ledger; only offline reward evidence and gameplay progress remain local-first.

AI Credit funds fal.ai-backed AI Artwork and can enter a Registered Account only through purchase or a Membership Credit Grant. Pattern completion, replay, daily activity, advertising rewards, Premium daily rewards, and real-money Stitch Coin Packs feed the separate Stitch Coin economy and never mint AI Credit. The first-release United States Stitch Coin Packs grant 300 Coin for `$1.99`, 900 for `$4.99`, or 2,000 for `$9.99`; they remain independent of AI Credit with no exchange between balances. Only a Registered Account may purchase a pack, while Guest Stitch Coin is held in the installation-scoped, server-authoritative Guest Ledger defined by ADR-0026.

Advertising is limited to Rewarded Ads that players explicitly start outside active Stitching Sessions; each verified completion grants 10 Stitch Coin, and the game has no forced interstitial ads or banners. One server-authoritative Ad-Equivalent Coin Pool contains 30 Coin per Reward Day and is shared by ads and the Premium Daily Coin Claim. Each verified ad consumes and grants 10, up to three. A Premium claim grants only the unconsumed remainder and then closes the pool: it grants 30 before any ad, 20 or 10 after one or two, and nothing after exhaustion. Thus joining Premium mid-day never removes Coin already earned, but no ordering can exceed 30. Each Reward Day begins at `00:00 UTC`; the client displays the remaining time and does not infer reset eligibility from device time.

Every Reward Day also offers three simultaneous Daily Tasks worth 10 Stitch Coin each: complete 100 Stitch Actions, complete at least 10 Stitch Actions in each of three DMC Thread Colors, and achieve one Thread Color Completion. Catalog, Personal, and Replay Sessions may count; the tasks never require ads, purchases, AI generation, or sharing. Completion grants automatically when online, while an offline completion persists as a Pending Coin Reward beyond the Reward Day boundary and is not spendable until backend reconciliation. The record carries an idempotent source key, Session and Pattern references, and a monotonic gameplay-event sequence. The backend owns day and Pattern uniqueness, reward caps, and impossible-transition or velocity checks; it grants valid evidence once and rejects client claims that cannot be reconciled. Incomplete tasks expire without streak loss or a missed-day penalty.

A First Completion Reward is granted automatically and exactly once per eligible Official Pattern or Community Pattern; an offline completion uses the same Pending Coin Reward path. Unlimited Personal Patterns never mint it, and Replay Sessions do not repeat it, although either may advance separately defined quests or events. Completion Reward Tiers use total stitchable cells only: Small grants 25 Stitch Coin for 1–3,999 cells, Medium grants 60 for 4,000–14,999, and Large grants 120 for 15,000 or more. DMC Thread Color count is not a reward multiplier. Official Pattern Unlock prices use the same ranges and are fixed at 75 Stitch Coin for Small, 150 for Medium, and 300 for Large without popularity-based dynamic pricing. Premium Membership never grants temporary catalog access; every player acquires a permanent Pattern Unlock through the same online backend-authorized Coin spend. Each Registered Account may receive Guest Coin, Pattern Unlocks, and pending reward value through one lifetime Guest Economy Promotion; later Guest Data-Only Promotions transfer progress, completion history, and Likes without economic value.

Stitch Coin is spent primarily on one-time, permanent Pattern Unlocks for Official Patterns, while a substantial starting selection remains free and core play or accessibility capabilities are never coin-gated. Community Patterns remain free in the first release, avoiding creator revenue-sharing expectations and pricing manipulation. Offline evidence validation cannot make a compromised client perfectly trustworthy, but the currency is non-transferable, cannot cash out, and remains bounded by server-owned daily, per-Pattern, and source-key limits. We accept that residual rooted-device risk instead of making all Coin earning online-only. This creates a second economy, an anonymous Guest Ledger, and additional reconciliation logic, but prevents ordinary edited local balances from authorizing private artifacts while allowing play to remain rewarding offline.

The fixed prices and guaranteed Daily Task income define Unlock Earnability Target without requiring ads, Premium, purchases, or a Player Level system: 30 Daily Task Coin funds Small within three Reward Days, Medium within five, and Large within ten. First Completion Rewards and optional Rewarded Ads shorten those upper bounds. Economy tuning may add value, but raising prices or lowering guaranteed task income beyond these bounds requires an explicit decision update rather than a silent live-ops change.
