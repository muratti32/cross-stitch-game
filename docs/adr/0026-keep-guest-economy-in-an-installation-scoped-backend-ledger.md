# Keep guest economy in an installation-scoped backend ledger

Each Guest Player receives an opaque Guest Installation Identity without providing email, Apple, or Google credentials. The Game Backend owns a Guest Ledger under that identity containing the guest's Stitch Coin balance, idempotent reward grants and spends, and permanent Pattern Unlocks. The client cannot mutate that ledger directly. Stitch Coin spending and new Pattern Unlocks require connectivity, allowing Session Preparation to authorize paid Official Pattern artifacts from a backend entitlement rather than trusting editable device storage.

The guest identity is installation-bound and has no supported recovery on another device or after its credential is lost. Before the first Stitch Coin spend and first Pattern Unlock, the client displays a non-blocking Guest Data Risk Notice explaining that the ledger, local progress, and Likes may become inaccessible and offering signup or sign-in. The player may dismiss it and continue without registration; registration remains the durability and portability boundary rather than a gameplay gate.

A Guest Player may choose online Guest Data Reset. After destructive confirmation, the backend closes the Guest Installation Identity and Guest Ledger, invalidates its credential and server session references, and the device deletes that identity's entire Local Identity Namespace before creating a fresh Guest identity. Coin, Unlocks, pending rewards, progress, Likes, and Offline Pattern Data are not transferred and cannot be recovered. Reset is distinct from sign-out, cache clearing, and Guest Data Promotion.

Gameplay remains local-first. An offline Daily Task or eligible first completion creates a Pending Coin Reward that is not spendable and survives Reward Day boundaries. On reconnection the backend validates its source key and grants it exactly once to the Guest Ledger. Stitching Session readiness and progress remain on the guest installation, although the backend may hold a minimal session identity needed for idempotent grants and preparation. Pattern Likes also remain local until promotion.

After signup or sign-in authenticates the target account, the device supplies a checksummed Guest Promotion Manifest of stable local item identifiers and versions plus complete validation evidence for every Pending Coin Reward. Before changing either identity, the backend validates that evidence and returns Guest Promotion Preview identifying economy or data-only mode and itemizing Guest Coin, Pattern Unlocks, validated or rejected Pending Coin Rewards, sessions, progress, completion history, and Likes that will transfer, remain locked, or be discarded. Confirmation is bound to the guest identity, target account, relevant backend state versions, reward-validation version, and manifest checksum; changed state or validation requires a new preview. Before sending confirmation, the device durably records Promotion Handoff with the preview and manifest versions. After confirmation the backend acquires Promotion Commit Lock and the client remains in the transfer flow while uploading an immutable, checksummed Promotion Transfer Package containing the complete local records needed for the approved dispositions but no Offline Pattern Data. Only a package that passes schema, identity, checksum, manifest-completeness, target-state, reward-evidence, and preview-freshness validation may proceed to commit. Cancellation, failure, or lock expiry before commit releases the lock, returns to the unchanged Guest session, and lets temporary staging expire.

Only confirmed Guest Data Promotion consumes the Guest Installation Identity. Its serializable compare-and-swap commit verifies that identity is unconsumed and every relevant target version still matches the preview. For the target account's one lifetime Guest Economy Promotion, the same commit adds the remaining Guest Ledger balance, unions Pattern Unlocks, validates and grants eligible pending rewards, deduplicates completion-reward source keys, closes the Guest Ledger, and consumes the account's economic-promotion slot. If a concurrent promotion or Unlock change makes the preview stale, the backend rejects it and requires a new preview and player confirmation; it never silently changes a confirmed economy promotion into data-only. A confirmed Guest Data-Only Promotion closes the Guest Ledger and acknowledges the discard of its balance, Unlocks, pending rewards, and other economic value in that same commit, while its staged package later merges session progress, completion history, and Pattern Likes. A data-only promoted session for a non-free Official Pattern without a target-account Unlock becomes Promotion-Locked: progress and history remain, device-local Offline Pattern Data is deleted, and artifact grants stop until the account independently acquires the Unlock. Session Preparation then reuses the preserved session identity.

Every promotion applies Promotion Session Merge by Pattern. Two active sessions use Progress Merge and retain the target account's active identity; one completed plus one active retains the completed history and continues the active attempt as a Replay Session; two completed attempts both remain in history. Completion and First Completion Reward eligibility are unique by target account and Pattern. Stable identifiers, merged tombstones, and idempotent upserts prevent duplicate grants or active sessions, and the consumed guest identity cannot spend, unlock, or promote again.

After backend commit, the promotion is irreversible: the consumed guest identity cannot be reactivated and failure does not roll the player back to Guest mode. Every Coin, Unlock, pending-reward, completion-reward, and economic-slot disposition is already durably resolved and acknowledged by that commit. The target account is immediately usable while the backend processes only the package's remaining progress, completion-history, and Like records independently of the source device. The local Promotion Handoff resumes status reconciliation after app restarts and retains each local record until an idempotent backend operation acknowledges its preview-approved transfer or intentional discard. Promotion-Locked Offline Pattern Data is deleted after commit and before target-account gameplay, while its progress record remains in the staged package. The client marks unresolved items as Syncing, requests automatic retries, provides a manual retry action, and removes the handoff only after every item is acknowledged. App deletion or device loss after commit cannot erase the staged transfer data.

Uncommitted promotion staging is removed immediately after cancellation when possible and always within 24 hours. A consumed, reset, or otherwise closed Guest Ledger retains detailed support and fraud evidence for 90 days, subject to a longer legal or active-investigation hold, then deletes that detail while preserving only a minimal non-replayable consumed-identity and promotion tombstone. This tombstone can prevent a closed identity from spending or promoting again but cannot reconstruct its balance, Unlocks, or personal data.

This decision supersedes the device-local Guest Stitch Coin and Pattern Unlock portions of ADR-0007 and ADR-0011. We accept an anonymous backend record, secure credential storage, online spending, bounded guest-ledger retention, explicit data-loss messaging, destructive reset, and promotion reconciliation in exchange for preventing edited local balances from authorizing private artifacts or being laundered into a durable account. It does not turn the guest into a recoverable cross-device account; registration remains the recovery and portability boundary.
