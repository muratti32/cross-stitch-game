# Keep creator blocks private and player-specific

A Registered Account may create or remove a Creator Block against a Public Creator Profile. While active, the block filters that creator and their Community Patterns out of the blocking player's catalog search, discovery, and recommendations across signed-in devices. It does not change global catalog state, create a moderation report, notify the blocked creator, or invalidate an existing Stitching Session; the blocking player may continue an already-started session.

We accept account-scoped block storage, per-player catalog filtering, synchronization across devices, and the possibility that blocked content remains accessible through an existing session in exchange for an immediate private safety and preference control that cannot be weaponized as global moderation. Reporting and blocking remain independent actions: a player may use either or both, but neither silently creates the other.

Account Deletion Finalization removes blocks owned by or targeting the deleted account as ordinary private relationship data. It creates no report, restriction, or notification, and Deleted Creator is not a blockable profile.
