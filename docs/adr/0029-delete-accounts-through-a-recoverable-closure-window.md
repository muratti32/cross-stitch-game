# Delete accounts through a recoverable closure window

Account Deletion Request requires recent reauthentication and enters Deletion Recovery Window for 30 days instead of immediately erasing an identity with paid value and public content. The backend revokes ordinary device sessions, blocks new player-initiated purchases and account mutations, stops new Processing Jobs, and prevents new Guest Data Promotion into the account. A job not yet submitted to its external provider is cancelled and releases its reservation. A fal.ai job already submitted cannot be treated as cancelled merely because the client or account closed: provider reconciliation remains a permitted system write during the window until terminal AI Artwork Delivery captures the reservation or proven terminal failure releases it. Delivered artwork remains private during closure, becomes visible if deletion is cancelled, and is deleted at finalization. An exceptionally unresolved provider job does not extend the deletion deadline; finalization reduces it to a minimal reconciliation tombstone, extinguishes its account reservation with the deleted balance, and discards any late output without creating AI Artwork, credit, or a claim for a later account. Required store notifications, reversals, deletion work, and already-committed promotion reconciliation likewise may write audit state. Account Closure Hold removes its Public Creator Profile, Community Patterns, links, and new session starts from public surfaces without mutating immutable bytes. Reauthentication during the window may cancel deletion and restore the same public identifiers; it cannot create new activity until the request is cancelled.

Committed Promotion Handoff remains irreversible during the window. Its staged package continues to resolve under the closing account and can enter Promotion Needs Attention, but no failure reactivates the consumed Guest identity. A final deletion instruction may intentionally discard any still-unresolved non-economic staged payload and acknowledge that disposition. Uncommitted promotions are cancelled. Pending Catalog Submissions, metadata revisions, and appeals accept no new action while closure is pending.

When Deletion Recovery Window ends, Account Deletion Finalization deletes Auth Identities and private profile data, AI Artwork, Personal Patterns, synchronized progress, Likes, Creator Blocks, and ordinary private account records; remaining virtual currency, credits, and Unlocks are forfeited without transfer or automatic refund. Like aggregates are decremented. Every Community Pattern receives Catalog Withdrawal, so new discovery, links, metadata revisions, and session starts end while existing Stitching Sessions retain the same limited play rights and Catalog Publication License as any other withdrawal. Public attribution becomes a non-interactive Deleted Creator tombstone, and the former username and opaque profile identifier remain reserved rather than being reassigned. Pending submissions, reviews, revisions, and ordinary appeals close; active safety or legal evidence follows its stronger retention requirement.

The backend retains detailed pseudonymous commerce, moderation, security, deletion, and idempotency evidence only for its bounded legal, fraud, support, or accounting period. Separate minimal one-way provider-transaction, consumed-identity, username-reservation, and opaque-profile-reservation tombstones remain for the life of the service; they prevent regrant or identifier reuse but cannot reconstruct deleted profile or purchase detail. A new signup creates an unrelated Registered Account and cannot reclaim those transactions, balances, content, username, or the deleted account's one-time Guest Economy Promotion history. The deletion flow explicitly warns that Apple or Google controls subscription cancellation and opens platform subscription management; deleting the game account cannot cancel the store contract.

Connected devices delete or cryptographically invalidate their Local Identity Namespace when they receive finalization. A fully offline device cannot be remotely erased at that instant, so its revoked namespace is removed at the next app or backend check; the user may perform Local Data Removal immediately on the device. We accept a grace-state model, reversible public hiding, withdrawal fan-out, delayed offline-device cleanup, username and transaction tombstones, and minimal audit retention in exchange for recoverability from accidental deletion, privacy-respecting final erasure, stable existing sessions, and no commerce or Guest-economy laundering.
