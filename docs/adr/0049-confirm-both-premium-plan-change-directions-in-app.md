# Confirm both Premium Plan change directions in the app, cancel a scheduled one in the store

A player who holds an `active` or `trial` Premium Membership on iOS commits to a Premium Plan change from one in-app confirmation, whichever direction it runs: the Commerce Store classifies the target against the `Premium Membership` subscription-group ordering of ADR-0043, states whether the change is immediate or deferred, and only then purchases the target package through RevenueCat. An upgrade activates at once and is verified against the new active plan; a downgrade is accepted by the store as a deferred change and is verified against the Scheduled Plan Change the Membership read model projects, never against the active plan. Cancelling an already-scheduled change stays with Manage Subscription — the app offers no in-app cancellation for it.

The confirmation is symmetric because the decision is the game's, not the store's: routing a downgrade straight to Manage Subscription left the player with no statement of what the change costs, when it takes effect, and no `subscription_change_started` to reconcile the store's eventual answer against — the app could represent the result of a change it could not start. The verification target differs by direction because the store's behaviour differs: a deferred downgrade grants nothing now, so waiting for a new active plan would leave every Premium action locked behind a reconciliation that cannot resolve until the period ends. Cancellation stays in the store because a pending plan change lives in the App Store subscription record: the app can request a change but has no authority to withdraw one the store already accepted, and offering a button that cannot guarantee the outcome would be worse than naming the surface that can.

## Consequences

- A confirmed downgrade emits `subscription_change_started` and, once the Scheduled Plan Change is visible, neither `purchase_completed` nor `subscription_change_completed`: nothing has been granted yet. `subscription_change_completed` fires later, from the activation the Game Backend observes at the next renewal (ADR-0050), so a change requested on one device is recorded exactly once no matter how many devices the player holds. `purchase_completed` is not fired for it at all: the renewal grants the ordinary Membership Credit for a period, not a purchase the player made.
- Dismissing the in-app confirmation emits `subscription_change_cancelled`, the same kind the store sheet's own cancellation emits. The two are distinguished by whether the store was reached at all, not by the event.
- Every Premium Plan action stays locked while a plan change is reconciling, downgrade included, so a second change cannot overlap in the subscription group before the first settles.
- Android has no single-group ordering to classify against, so it keeps Manage Subscription as its only plan-change surface and this ADR does not apply there.
- If Apple ever exposes a first-party API to withdraw a pending plan change, the cancellation half of this decision is what would be revisited; the confirmation half is independent of it.
