I got this app because I love stitching Owl Forest designs which aren’t fully compatible with the other markup apps and this app is designed for use with SAGA files. It’s a brilliant app that makes following a pattern so much easier and more enjoyable. It’s elegant and easy to use at its basic level and there are loads of customization options to make following the pattern easier for one’s individual style.
