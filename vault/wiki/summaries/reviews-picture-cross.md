### waiting4nextupdate (2026-07-06) - 1★ [App Store]
> **Update ruined**
I have had this game for 8 years and I used to play it every day to bank green tokens for future puzzle releases. The latest update switched the reward system. Now it costs green tokens to replay levels and the reward are yellow coins. The coins do not help you at all unless you need clues.

### Stove210 (2026-07-06) - 1★ [App Store]
> **Ads are taking over**
Ads were fine on the app and it’s a good way to keep it free, but some of the advertisements start automatically playing video in full screen WHILE YOU ARE PLAYING A PUZZLE. Makes the game more frustrating and annoying.

### Edjordan63 (2026-07-04) - 5★ [App Store]
> **Audio ads**
It used to be a relaxing game, now audio ads suddenly blare out whatever they’re trying to sell. No way to stop the annoying mid-game ads other than turn down the sound on your device. Other than that, it’s an alright game.
Update: The developer reached out to me and showed me how to report the ads that were causing the issue. It’s been a couple of days since then, and no more audio ads during gameplay.

### Rockg:rl (2026-07-02) - 2★ [App Store]
> **Could have been fun**
This could have been so much more fun without the ads interfering. I don’t have money to pay to go “ad free”. I wanted a fun game to unwind with. Instead there are coin limits, time limits, and lots of ads. I only recently downloaded this game. But because of the excessive ad interference I will be removing this game from my phone.
