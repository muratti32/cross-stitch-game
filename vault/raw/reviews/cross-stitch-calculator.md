### donahue73 (2019-04-25) - 5★ [App Store]
- **Version:** 1.6
- **Comment:** **Go-to XStitch Calculator**
Love this app on my iPad Pro. I regularly update my software and have yet to experience any problems with the app not working.

One reviewer said the app didn’t calculate correctly, but I disagree. If you have a 4x4 project and want a 3-inch border all around, the fabric size would need to be 10x10, not 7x7. (Remember, you’re adding 3 inches to ALL sides: 3+4+3=10.)

Because none of us want to run short on fabric in our final stitched piece, the app rounds up. I like this feature. I’ve been burned too many times when I tried to save money by skimping on fabric. Better to spend a little money than waste a lot of time (and money) finding out too late that you cut your fabric too small.
