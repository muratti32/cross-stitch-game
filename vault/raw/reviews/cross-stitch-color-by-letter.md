### kanuch (2022-08-03) - 3★ [App Store]
- **Version:** 1.0.6
- **Comment:** **Some great mechanics, terrible color mapping**
There are definitely some things to like about this cross stitch app that make it better than some others I’ve played, but there are a couple painful flaws, too.  
Good: responsive to touch, but not too responsive.  Great error fixing approach.  Being able to zoom in and out and color at any level.  Being able to move with one finger but also being able to lock that down so that you can focus on one region.

Bad:  two things, really.  #1:  there is a clickbait button in the top right corner that you can watch something to get coins. This is on the screen and very easy to accidentally touch.  When you do so you are “rewarded” with what seems to be 90 seconds of crap that there is no way to click out of and forgo the wonderful coins (I have no idea what the coins are for, either.  So you might be cruising along enjoying yourself and accidentally brush that stupid button and then you might as well get up to go make a cup of coffee because your iPad is seized by the ad.  Very annoying.
#2:  the worst thing, really… love the ability to import pictures to color yourself, this is what I mostly do in the app, I download pictures and then chill out while coloring them.  Unfortunately the algorithm used by the app for mapping colors is just terrible, and every picture looks incredibly washed out.  I usually do black-and-white pictures to get around this, but it is a major flaw.  Love to see these two things addressed.
