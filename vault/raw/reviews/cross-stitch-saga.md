### KittyMom04 (2025-10-25) - 5★ [App Store]
- **Version:** 5.0.6
- **Comment:** **An extraordinarily well designed app**
I got this app because I love stitching Owl Forest designs which aren’t fully compatible with the other markup apps and this app is designed for use with SAGA files. It’s a brilliant app that makes following a pattern so much easier and more enjoyable. It’s elegant and easy to use at its basic level and there are loads of customization options to make following the pattern easier for one’s individual style. 

Probably the most challenging aspect of it is just getting a handle on all the options. Documentation is good, although the descriptions of the different tools and their options are so succinct that I found it hard to keep track of everything. There are links to video tutorials, but they too are short and with no verbal explanation so they can be difficult to follow.

Read the developer’s description thoroughly before purchasing. This app does NOT support the importing of PDF’s — you must have one of the file formats she notes in the description. This is not an app for designing — it is a markup and tracking app to make the stitching process simpler and that it does better than any other markup app for either iOS or Android.
