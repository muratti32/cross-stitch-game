### marci.nyc (2021-01-08) - 4★ [App Store]
- **Version:** 2.0.16
- **Comment:** **With Some Tweaks, Can Be 5 Stars**
First off, let me say I didn’t expect to like this app but I do! I downloaded it to use for the free Shooting Stars pattern after struggling with the .jpg files they offered via their blog. 

I don’t really care about tracking my stitches per day or hours stitched — if that’s your thing, this app calculates it for you. I do like how it’s easy to “highlight” what you’ve stitched (something I do on physical charts).  I also like that there are fabric size calculations and estimates for amounts of floss needed based on fabric count but I wish it would calculate for 28 ct linen (currently stops at 32 ct). 

There are a variety of charts available for purchase from a small selection of European designers. I’m undecided if I’ll make future purchases thru the app or order physical copies. There is a print option via the app if you feel the need to print a hard copy. 

What keeps this from being a five-star app is the lack of syncing between devices. I have been using my iPad to work on a project but there are times I don’t want to schlepp it along with me. It’d be nice if I could open the app on my phone and have my progress synced. At this point, it’s not worth having the app on multiple devices.
