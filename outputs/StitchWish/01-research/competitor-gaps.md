## 4. Positioning opportunities (ranked)

1. **AI plus photo as the lead message.** Nobody in the direct set pairs AI art with stitching (0/8 for AI). Photo is the proven demand signal, so the pairing is: photo-to-pattern for the stitcher's own pictures, AI art when they have none. Use it in the subtitle or promo text, and in the first screenshot. Needs validation: volume for "ai cross stitch" and whether AI-generated art is acceptable for the target audience.
2. **Real-world stitchable, proven.** Two rivals claim DMC, and D8 also offers a printable PDF after completion. Our claim is weaker unless we show it: a DMC-mapped palette list or a printable chart export (product question, not a copy fix). Without export, do not out-claim D8. Match it with proof.
3. **Calm, offline, no-pressure as a set.** No direct rival combines offline with no timers. But D8 owns "no timers, no lives, no fail states" wording. Position as "play offline, no pressure", and avoid the exact D8 phrase.
4. **Creator profile and community catalog.** Only adjacent apps (A1, A2) have community language at scale. Among direct apps this is open. It is a retention and discovery story, not a search term. Lower priority for store copy.
5. **Defend "pixel" deliberately.** D8 frames "pixel" as childlike. Our keyword field and D1 title use pixel. Decide whether the stitch grid is the visual hook. If yes, show crisp grid screenshots that answer D8's realism claim. If no, drop "pixel" from the headline.

## 5. Pricing read-out (for positioning, not copy)

- Rivals sell weekly subscriptions at $3.49 (D3), $5.99 (D6), $7.99 (D1 premium), plus monthly plans at $14.99 to $19.99 (D8, D4 VIP, D6). Free trials confirmed for D1, D6 and adjacent A2. D3 and D8 trial terms were not captured.
- D7 Stitchly is pay-once, $39.99 to $75.00, and is the only "no subscription" pitch. Good pitch for stitch-tool buyers.
- Our IAP range per store page is $1.99 to $39.99 (Premium Annual $39.99). We cannot compare plans directly without our subscription table. Mark as unknown until checked.
